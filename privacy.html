Privacy Policy

ZXBOT. Effective October 2026.

Who uses this app: only the Operator, the creator of the Botanical Oddities channel.

Data accessed: the Operator's TikTok authorisation token, used to upload the Operator's own videos to the Operator's own drafts.

Where it is stored: only on the Operator's own computer, encrypted.

Data shared: none. No data is sold, shared or sent to anyone other than TikTok's own API.

Data about other TikTok users: none is collected.

Retention and deletion: the token is deleted when the Operator disconnects the app or removes it from the computer. The Operator can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings.
